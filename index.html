How to interpret

Risk probability

Represents the model-estimated probability that the patient will fail to achieve the corresponding MCID 1 year after surgery. A threshold of 0.50 is the prespecified classification threshold for the study, not a threshold for surgical indication.

SHAP contribution

Shows the direction and magnitude of each predictor's contribution to this prediction. To protect patient privacy, the public website uses synthetic reference samples generated from the training-cohort distribution to calculate SHAP. Changing the reference background may alter the SHAP baseline and individual predictor contributions but will not change the RF prediction probability.

Scope of application

The model underwent same-center prospective temporal validation but still lacks geographic external validation and a clinical impact trial. It is intended only for research and supplementary risk communication.

Important: This tool only provides a reference for patient prognosis and cannot replace treatment decisions.
